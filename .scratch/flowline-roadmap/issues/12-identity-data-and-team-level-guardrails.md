# Identity data and team-level guardrails

Type: grilling (HITL)
Status: open
Blocked by: 03
Label: wayfinder:grilling

## Question

What identity data does Flowline store at all, and what does team-level only mean concretely in the model, the API and the UI? Decide whether events carry a person identifier, whether pseudonymization is the default or an option, the smallest group size a metric may be shown for, and the wording of the documented principle. Pseudonymization ships in v0.4, but what the event store holds must be decided before v0.1 writes its first row.
