# Services and the second connector

Type: grilling (HITL)
Status: open
Blocked by: 02

## Question

Is Azure DevOps Services a mode of the Azure DevOps connector or a connector of its own, and at which milestone is it supported? The roadmap puts a second connector in v1.0 while the non-goals exclude GitHub, GitLab and Jira from v1; decide what the v1.0 second connector is, or remove it from v1.0.
